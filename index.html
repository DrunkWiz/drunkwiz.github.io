How I like to build

  • Put the model where judgement is needed, and keep it out where correctness is needed.
  • Degrade instead of breaking. A second model provider, a second map, offline data underneath.
  • Tests run with no network and no API key.
  • Say plainly what's real and what's simulated.

Who I picture

For three years in school, I went out every quarter with a volunteer group to visit elderly residents, in their own neighbourhoods and at homes for the aged, bringing games and food to them.

When I decide what goes on a small screen, they're who I picture. If it works for them, it usually works for everyone else too.
